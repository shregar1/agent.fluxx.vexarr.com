We can't depend on external SaaS. What if the internet is down? What if the vendor is having an outage during a disaster?

## Maria and Team, Venezuela

"Internet is unreliable here. We built a community mesh network connecting 5,000 homes.

We needed to monitor the network health. Which nodes are up? Where's the congestion? Where do we need more coverage?

Commercial tools are impossible—they need reliable internet and credit cards that work internationally.

Fluxx Agent runs on a Raspberry Pi in our community center. It monitors everything. It works offline. It costs nothing.

We connected people who had no voice. This tool helped us keep that connection alive."
