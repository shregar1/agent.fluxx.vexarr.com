# The Fluxx Manifesto: Observability is a Right, Not a Privilege

**Published:** April 2026  
**Reading time:** 6 minutes  
**Who this is for:** Every developer who's been priced out of understanding their own software

---

## The Problem We Believe In

Every developer deserves to know what their software is doing.

Not just developers at Google. Not just developers with $18M budgets. Every developer.

The current state of observability is broken:

- **A student** building their first web app can't afford to see their logs
- **A startup** with $100K funding has to choose between observability and hiring
- **A non-profit** serving millions can't debug why their site is down
- **A developer in Nigeria** pays 10× their monthly salary for monitoring

This is wrong.

---

## Our Beliefs

### 1. Observability is Infrastructure, Not Luxury

Just like you need a text editor to write code, you need observability to run software.

It shouldn't cost more than the servers you're monitoring.

### 2. Data About Your Software Belongs to You

Not to a vendor in San Francisco. Not locked in a proprietary format. You.

### 3. Knowledge Should Be Free

The techniques for high-performance monitoring aren't trade secrets. They're engineering principles that should be shared, improved, and available to all.

### 4. Community Beats Corporation

A thousand developers building together will always create something better than a hundred employees building for profit.

### 5. Performance is Accessibility

If observability only works at scale with big budgets, it's not real observability. It needs to work on a $5 VPS. It needs to work on a Raspberry Pi.

---
